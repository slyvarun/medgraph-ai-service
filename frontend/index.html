⚕️ MedGraph Nexus — Architecture & Regulatory Guidelines

Clinical Decision Support (CDS) Architecture:

  • Split-Screen Verification: Synthesized AI reasoning is presented alongside raw, unedited openFDA and RxNorm database entries so HCPs can independently verify recommendations.
  • Deterministic Graph Traversal: Relationships follow verified paths: [Original Drug] → [Ingredient] → [Symptom] ← [Alternative Drug].
  • Granular Footnote Citations: Clickable numeric citations [1], [2] directly bind AI assertions to regulatory labels.
  • Strict Guardrails: Unknown symptoms and fabricated entities trigger immediate standard refusal without unanchored speculation.
