🌿 Why MedGraph Nexus Exists — Our Mission

A Free Healthcare Intelligence Initiative: Navigating prescription drugs in the United States and globally is complex and unnecessarily expensive. Over $500B is spent annually on brand-name medications when identical, bioequivalent generic molecules exist at a fraction of the cost.

🎯 The Three Core Goals of MedGraph Nexus:

  1. Unmasking Generic Bioequivalents: Helping patients and doctors find approved generic alternatives to high-cost branded medications.
  2. Zero-Hallucination Safety: Bypassing black-box LLM guessing by strictly anchoring all answers in verified federal NIH RxNorm chemical structures and FDA package labels.
  3. 100% Privacy & Universal Access: Zero advertisements, zero pharmaceutical sponsor influence, and no user profile data collection.

MedGraph Nexus is developed for educational and clinical decision support purposes under FDA 2026 CDS guidance. All data originates from public health repositories (NIH RxNorm, FDA CDER).

⚕️ MedGraph Nexus — CDS Architecture & Compliance

Clinical Decision Support (CDS) Architecture:

  • Split-Screen Verification: Synthesized AI reasoning is presented alongside raw, unedited openFDA and RxNorm database entries so clinicians and patients can independently verify recommendations.
  • Deterministic Graph Traversal: Relationships follow verified paths: [Original Drug] → [Ingredient] → [Symptom] ← [Alternative Drug].
  • Granular Footnote Citations: Clickable numeric citations [1], [2] directly bind AI assertions to regulatory labels.
  • Anti-Hallucination Guardrails: Unknown symptoms and fabricated entities trigger immediate refusal without unanchored speculation.
